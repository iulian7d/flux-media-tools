How Loan Payments Are Calculated

Monthly payments are calculated using the standard amortization formula, where each payment covers interest on the remaining balance plus a portion of principal. Early payments are mostly interest; later payments shift toward principal.

Adding even a small extra payment each month can significantly reduce total interest and shorten the loan term — especially in the early years of the loan.
