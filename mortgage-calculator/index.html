How to Use This Calculator

Mortgage Affordability: Enter your income, existing debts, and down payment to find the maximum home price you can comfortably afford based on a 28%/36% DTI guideline. The monthly payment shown includes principal, interest, taxes, and insurance (PITI).

Loan Calculator: Enter any loan amount, rate, and term to get your exact monthly payment and a full amortization schedule. Add extra monthly payments to see how much interest you save and how early you pay off the loan.
