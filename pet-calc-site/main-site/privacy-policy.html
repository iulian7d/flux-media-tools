Who We Are

Paw Calculators (pawcalculators.com and its subdomains) provides free pet health and nutrition calculators for dog and cat owners. We are not veterinarians and do not provide medical advice.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information you enter into our calculators. All calculations are performed locally in your browser — no input data is transmitted to our servers. We do not require any registration or login.
